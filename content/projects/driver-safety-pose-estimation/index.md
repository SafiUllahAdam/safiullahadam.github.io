My Bachelor's final-year project, **Driver Safety using Human Pose Estimation**, was selected among the **Top 15 National AI Projects** by Pakistan's IGNITE Innovation Fund, received national funding, and was featured on Pakistani national television.

<!--more-->

I later extended the work into a full research publication (presented at the 2nd International Conference on Emerging Trends — ICET, currently under review), integrating a **YOLOv8-based real-time pose estimation pipeline** with FPN-style feature fusion and a lightweight attention mechanism that focuses on safety-critical regions such as the eyes, head, and hands.

Using the **DriPE dataset**, I analysed inter-joint dependencies to perform fatigue and drowsiness classification — strengthening my expertise in video-based behaviour recognition, structured representation learning from keypoints, and domain robustness.
