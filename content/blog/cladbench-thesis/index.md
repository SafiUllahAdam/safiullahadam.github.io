I have started my Master 2 research thesis at the **LIRIS laboratory** (GOAL team), under the supervision of **Prof. Hamida Seba**.

The project, **CLADBench**, is an open-source, publication-grade benchmark for Collaborative Learning in Graph Anomaly Detection. It unifies the ADBench, GADBench, and PyGOD ecosystems under a single collaborative learning framework built around a pseudo-label exchange mechanism between heterogeneous detectors.

More updates to follow as the work progresses.
