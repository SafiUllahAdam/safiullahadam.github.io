abstract: >-
  We present a real-time driver fatigue and drowsiness detection system based
  on human pose estimation. A YOLOv8-based pose estimation pipeline is combined
  with FPN-style feature fusion and a lightweight attention mechanism that
  focuses on safety-critical regions such as the eyes, head, and hands. Using
  the DriPE dataset, we analyse inter-joint dependencies to classify driver
  fatigue and drowsiness, demonstrating robust video-based behaviour
  recognition from structured keypoint representations. The work originated as
  a Bachelor's final-year project selected among the Top 15 National AI
  Projects by Pakistan's IGNITE Innovation Fund.
